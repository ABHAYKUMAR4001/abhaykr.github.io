About

Hi, I'm Abhay Kumar — an engineer with 10+ years spanning automotive, rail transport and healthcare. My work is defined by architecting complex systems, leading multifunctional teams and delivering high-quality, safety-critical solutions.

Earlier I drove system interoperability at Baxter, digital cockpit & ADAS at Harman, and passenger communication systems at Alstom.

I'm deeply invested in AI-driven engineering — building GenAI tools that are reshaping how ADAS teams innovate, author requirements and validate designs. I hold a B.Tech in Electrical Engineering (NIT Srinagar), a PG Diploma in Cyber Security, and am pursuing an M.Tech in AI & ML at BITS Pilani.
